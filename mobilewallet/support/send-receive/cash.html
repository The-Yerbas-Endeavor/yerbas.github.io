Why my Yerbas cash Receive Address change?

Whenever you receive a payment to the Yerbas address shown, a new address is automatically generated for you.

By using a unique address for every transaction, you can increase privacy and security. Old addresses will still continue to work, even if they are no longer displayed in your app.
