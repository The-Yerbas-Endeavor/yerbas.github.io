How do I update my PIN?

Your YERB Wallet PIN helps keep your wallet secure and is needed to send transactions out of your wallet. For the best security, we recommend you create a PIN that is different from your device PIN. If you believe your PIN may have been compromised or just wish to change this to a new one, you can update this by following these steps: After logging in to the YERB Wallet, tap on Menu. Select Security Settings. Choose Update PIN. Enter your current PIN Enter the new PIN and confirm this on the next page. Your PIN can only be reset by using your 12-word Recovery Phrase.
